<objective>
Read the audit run's findings back from the live sidecar and prove two integrity
properties on real output: no finding reaches `confirmed` on Tier-2-only evidence, and
runtime-dependent findings land in `needs-deployment-testing` with a real risk score
visible in the report headline counts.

This expands Plan 02's audit run outward into the finding-quality layer, reusing the
receipt format and sanitization gate the Plan 01 tracer established.

Purpose: satisfies AUD-02 and AUD-03 against real findings rather than fixtures.
Output: a sanitized finding-integrity receipt and ledger rows for every violation.
</objective>
