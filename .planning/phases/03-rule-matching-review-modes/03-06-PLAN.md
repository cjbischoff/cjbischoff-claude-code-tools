<objective>
Wire a real finding source into the review verb. The resolved rule doc reaches a per-file
reviewing agent, the agent's comments become findings, and those findings flow through the
position gate, the profile gate, the reflection filter, and the receipt gate.

Purpose: without this plan the review verb still calls `review_position_gate([], ...)` and
`review --profile general` on a real diff reports nothing, leaving phase success criterion 4
unreachable. The rule matching, profile, and reflection machinery built in plans 01-05 has no
input until this seam exists.
Output: `review_agent.py`, `agents/review-file.md`, the SKILL.md review-mode dispatch block,
and a live end-to-end profile-split test on a fixture diff.
</objective>
